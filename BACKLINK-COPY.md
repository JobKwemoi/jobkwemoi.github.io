## 4. Directories worth a listing (all free, all real backlinks)

- **Google Business Profile** (business.google.com) — biggest single
  lever for "Nairobi" / local-intent searches. List as a service-area
  business (you don't need a public office address). Category: "Website
  designer" or "Software company."
- **Clutch.co** or **GoodFirms** — freelance/agency directories, good
  domain authority, free basic listing.
- **Kenyan dev communities** — if you're in any WhatsApp/Slack/Discord
  groups for Nairobi developers, a pinned intro with your portfolio link
  counts too, informally.

## Why this over more landing pages right now

Your site is small and your current position (1.93 in Kenya) is already
strong. Backlinks from profiles you control add real authority without
touching your existing pages or risking keyword overlap between them —
lower risk, and usually faster to show up in Search Console than new
content has time to get crawled and trusted.

Revisit the bigger landing-page plan (Phase 2 from the SEO analysis)
once you've got a few weeks of data showing whether the CTR-optimized
title (already live) and these backlinks moved the numbers.

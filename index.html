MY OWN BUILD

Meet Kirong AI 👑

Kirong AI isn't just a chatbot — it's an autonomous agent platform I engineered myself. It plans and carries out multi-step tasks (research, coding, business strategy, content), writes and actually runs code in an isolated sandbox to verify it works, and pauses to ask your approval before anything risky — with a full undo/audit trail behind every action.

  • 🤖 Autonomous agents — Research, Coding, Business & Content agents that plan and execute multi-step tasks on their own
  • 🔒 Human-in-the-loop safety — every risky action pauses for your approval, with full undo and an audit trail
  • 🧪 Real code execution — writes, runs and fixes code in an isolated sandbox before calling a job done
  • 🛠️ Build your own agent — pick the tools and instructions for a custom Kirong agent tailored to your workflow
  • ⚡ Runs on a multi-provider AI stack (OpenAI, Groq, Gemini & more) so it stays fast even when one provider slows down

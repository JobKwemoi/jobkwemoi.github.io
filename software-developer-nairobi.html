Why "Nairobi-based" matters

A lot of software development work can happen from anywhere. But being based in Nairobi means I build with Kenyan realities in mind from the start: variable mobile data speeds, M-Pesa as the default payment method, WhatsApp as where customers actually want to talk to a business, and pricing that makes sense for a local SME budget rather than a Silicon Valley one.

It also means a same-timezone conversation, in person if it helps, and a developer who understands the business context without needing it explained from scratch.
